Refund & Cancellation Policy

HomTrus · Last updated: 24 November 2026

HomTrus connects customers with independent verified professionals. This policy explains how payment, cancellation and refunds work. All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹).

How payment works

You do not pay when you book. You pay after the professional has inspected the work, you have approved their quote, and the job is completed. You can pay online (UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay) or in cash to the professional.

Every visit has a platform visit charge of ₹49. It is shown in the app before you confirm.

Cancelling a booking

When you cancelWhat you pay
Before a professional accepts, after they accept, or while they are on the wayNothing.
After the professional has arrived, before you approve the quoteThe ₹49 visit charge.
You reject the quoteOnly the ₹49 visit charge. It is non-refundable.
After you approve the quote and work has startedCannot be cancelled in the app. Contact us at homtrus@gmail.com.

If a professional cancels or does not arrive

You pay nothing. You can book another professional at any time.

Problems with the service

Work paid through the app has a 30-day service warranty. If you are not happy with the work, email homtrus@gmail.com within 48 hours of completion with your booking ID and, if possible, photos. We will review it and may arrange a re-visit, a partial refund or a full refund, depending on what happened.

If you pay or settle outside the app, the warranty does not apply, so please pay and record payment in the app.

Coupons

A coupon discount is not refunded as cash. If a refund is approved, we refund the amount you actually paid.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They usually reach you in 5 to 7 working days, and your bank may take a little longer to show the credit. Refunds for cash payments are handled case by case, so contact us.

How to reach us

Email homtrus@gmail.com with your booking ID and registered mobile number. We reply within 2 working days.
