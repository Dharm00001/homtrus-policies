Privacy Policy

HomTrus · Last updated: 25 November 2026

HomTrus is an app that helps customers book verified home service professionals such as plumbers and electricians. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Part A is for customers, Part B is for professionals, and Part C applies to everyone.

HomTrus is run from Ludhiana, Punjab, India. Contact: homtrus@gmail.com.

Part A: Customers

What we collect

  • Mobile number, used to log you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Name and the address where you want the service.
  • Booking details, quotes, ratings and reviews.
  • Photos or videos you upload for a job.
  • Your location, only if you allow it, to detect your city, show nearby professionals, and show the job location on a map.
  • Basic device and crash information to keep the app working.

What the professional sees: once a professional accepts your booking, they see your name, address, job photos and location, and can call you during the booking. You see the professional's name, photo, skill and rating.

Part B: Professionals

What we collect

  • Mobile number, name, city, skill, years of experience and profile photo.
  • For verification: Aadhaar details (front, back and address), PAN, and a police clearance certificate.
  • A live selfie when you arrive at a job, compared with your registered photo to confirm it is you.
  • Earnings and wallet records, and payout details if you add them.

Consent before verification: we ask for your clear consent before we begin identity and background verification. If you do not consent, you can still use HomTrus as a customer, but you cannot take jobs as a professional.

Only what is needed: we ask only for the documents needed to confirm who you are and that you are safe to send to a customer's home. We do not ask for anything else.

Who sees your documents: Aadhaar, PAN and police clearance documents are never shown to customers. Only a small number of authorised HomTrus staff can open them, and only to verify you.

Part C: Everyone

Why we use your information

  • To create your account and log you in.
  • To match customers with professionals and complete bookings.
  • To verify professionals and keep customers safe, including the SOS feature.
  • To process payments, refunds and payouts.
  • To send booking updates and security messages by SMS or in-app notification.
  • To prevent fraud, resolve disputes and meet legal duties.

Payments

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or net-banking details. Razorpay processes them under its own privacy policy. We keep only the payment status and reference ID.

Who we share information with

  • The other party in your booking, as described above.
  • Service providers who help us run HomTrus: Razorpay (payments), an SMS provider (OTPs), and cloud hosting and database providers.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

We do not sell your personal information.

Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Our database provider encrypts stored data.
  • Access to servers, the database and the admin panel is restricted and protected by secret credentials.

No system is perfectly secure. Please never share your OTP with anyone.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information and your account.
  • Withdraw a consent you gave earlier.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

To do any of this, email homtrus@gmail.com from your registered contact with the subject "Privacy request". We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will act quickly to contain it, and we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

HomTrus is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
